# Hand-drawn A4 sketch guide

This text guide is a reference. It is not the hand-drawn sketch required for assessment.

Use one A4 portrait sheet to sketch the entire scrolling webpage. Put the short title, research question and three sector-value callouts at the top. Draw four clearly labelled sections in the same order as the implemented page.

1. **Growth:** indexed sector line chart and treemap in the first row; contribution waterfall and industry bump chart below. Label the value/output control and the index baseline.
2. **Geography:** a wide proportional-symbol Australia map with sector/year controls. Below it, draw the avocado yield choropleth beside the nursery Dorling cartogram. Put a legend under each and a short regional contrast annotation below the row.
3. **Output:** a wide industry-year heatmap. Below it, draw the output/value-change scatterplot beside the nursery plant-output bar chart. Label zero reference lines and write that nursery uses plant units.
4. **Markets and industry:** a wide potato Sankey with three branches. Below it, draw the export-share waffle beside the avocado bearing-area/business-count lines. End with a short conclusion and a source footer.

Mark two desktop columns, aligned chart edges, narrative text locations and source notes. Include the three distinct map idioms and their legends. Use brief annotations rather than trying to fit full article text on A4.

Photograph or scan the actual hand-drawn sheet, make a PDF and upload it to the repository. Obtain tutor feedback where possible. The final page and sketch should agree in topic, section order and visual structure.
